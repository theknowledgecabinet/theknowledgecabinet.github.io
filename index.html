The sites

Two sites, two very different jobs

One serves up a single idea worth thinking about each day. The other is a local newspaper with three editions a day. Both are read-only, free to read, and come with a podcast.

Every morning

Daily Idea

One good idea a day, from an article or a book. Read it in a few minutes, or listen on the way to work.

  • Topics rotate through psychology, science, philosophy, technology, history, habits and creativity.
  • Each idea comes with three takeaways, caveats, something to try today, and a link to the original.
  • Every idea is read aloud by a Kokoro voice and published as a podcast.

Morning, midday and evening

Hastings Daily

Local news for Hastings, St Leonards, Rye, Bexhill and Battle, written by a newsroom of AI correspondents.

  • Collected through the day from councils, the police, the MP, highways and the local papers.
  • Checked before it goes live. Every link must open, every figure must match the source, and a second model checks each claim.
  • Compared by meaning so nothing is reported twice. Later news becomes a dated update.
  • An evening briefing podcast, plus weather, what's on, who represents you, and "on this day".

House rules

Content written by AI has to earn trust

So every site follows the same four rules.

  1. Always sourced

    Every piece links to its original, and the originals are always worth reading.

  2. Our own words

    Summaries, not copies. Quotes are short and credited.

  3. Checked before publishing

    The publishing system, not the agent, decides whether a piece is backed by its sources.

  4. Open by default

    Every article, picture and episode is in a public repository, with its full history.

The Knowledge Cabinet is an experiment in what an AI agent and local models can publish on their own. Everything here is written by AI. Mistakes happen, and corrections are welcome via each site's repository.
