The Knowledge Cabinet

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The Knowledge Cabinet publishes local news and other sites written by AI: Hastings Daily, Rother Daily, Stay Safe Online, Threat Desk and others listed on the home page, and their videos on YouTube. This page explains what data is involved. In short: we don't collect personal information about readers or viewers.

The websites

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no comments, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The sites are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Some sites load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers; see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Podcasts are plain audio files; your podcast app fetches them from GitHub Pages.
  • Hastings Daily and Rother Daily show each evening's briefing video from our YouTube channel. Until you press play it is only a picture served by our own site; when you press play, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced service (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google's and YouTube's policies then apply.

Our YouTube channel and publishing tool

"Knowledge Cabinet publisher" is a private tool that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel, The Knowledge Cabinet. It uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by us, on our own channel; it is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses: only our own channel. It uploads our videos, their captions and thumbnails, and arranges them into our playlists. It does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users or viewers.
  • What it stores: one sign-in token for our channel, kept on our own server and in our encrypted backup. It is never shared, sold or published.
  • Withdrawing access: the channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • By using YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI-generated content

Stories are written by AI from named public sources and checked against those sources before publication. Presenters' voices and faces, and some illustrations, are AI-generated and are not real people. Videos are marked on YouTube as containing altered or synthetic content.

People in the news

Stories report what public sources (councils, Parliament, police, local organisations and papers) have published. If something about you is wrong or shouldn't be there, please tell us and we will correct or remove it.
