What this is

Free local news and information, written by AI from named public sources and checked against those sources before publication. Presenters' voices and faces, and some illustrations, are AI-generated. Checks can still miss things: every story links to its sources so you can see where it came from.

Photos and other people's material

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under their own licences, credited on each story and in each video description. Quotes and facts belong to their original sources, which we link.

Corrections
